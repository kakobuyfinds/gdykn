What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links and comparison notes for purchases being considered through Kakobuy. Useful entries record the item, seller, selected variant, size information, listed price and date checked. A list alone does not verify a seller or product. This guide helps you structure that research and browse relevant CAFANS categories.

03 / How to use a Kakobuy spreadsheet

A useful list does more
than collect links.

01 — DISCOVER

Choose a category and a purpose.

Begin with what you need: an everyday sneaker, a layering piece or a small accessory. Browse the matching category and save a manageable shortlist.

02 — COMPARE

Record the exact variant.

Keep the source link alongside the size, color, material description and date checked. Similar pictures can represent different variants, sellers or specifications.

03 — REVIEW

Resolve gaps before buying.

Recheck availability, measurements, return terms and the full cost. If you intend to use Kakobuy, confirm current link support and purchase eligibility directly with the service.

04 / Your comparison framework

Keep the details.
Lose the guesswork.

The most useful Kakobuy product list is one you can revisit and understand. Separate a seller's claims from your own observations, and mark unknown details instead of filling them in by assumption.

For a Kakobuy clothing spreadsheet, measurements deserve their own columns. For a Kakobuy shoes spreadsheet, record the sizing system and the exact selected size.

Photo check: distinguish presentation from evidence.

Promotional images show presentation. If inspection photos are available, examine the actual item, selected variant, visible condition and relevant measurements. Images alone cannot establish authenticity.

Budget check: compare the delivered cost.

A lower listed price may not mean a lower total. Include applicable shipping, service charges, conversion fees and destination taxes or duties before comparing alternatives.

06 / Straight answers

Before you
build your list.

Practical answers for researching product finds and keeping your comparisons useful.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links and comparison notes for purchases being considered through Kakobuy. Useful entries record the item, seller, selected variant, size information, listed price and date checked. A list alone does not verify a seller or product.

Is this page an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and comparison guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, including the category pages shown near the top.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides category links and a comparison framework. You can use the suggested fields to organize your own spreadsheet.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

Use the specific listing's size chart. For shoes, compare the stated foot-length guidance and sizing system. For clothing, compare garment measurements with a similar item you own, allowing for the seller's measurement method and stated tolerance.

Does a product photo confirm authenticity?

No. A photo, brand name or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity. Examine the seller's evidence, product documentation and applicable return terms before making a purchasing decision.

How do I estimate the total cost?

Record the item price and separately check any domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, payment or currency conversion fees, and destination taxes or duties. Which charges apply depends on the transaction and destination.
